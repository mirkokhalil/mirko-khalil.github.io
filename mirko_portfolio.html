About Me

Not just a designer.
A builder.

I'm Mirko Khalil Majeed — 24 years old, based in Sulaymaniyah, Kurdistan of Iraq. I started as a graphic designer and kept going. I didn't stop at design. I went into branding, marketing, strategy, quality management, entrepreneurship, real estate — because I'm not interested in being defined by one lane.

"Still building, creating, getting, doing anything that gets me to my goal — financial freedom. Energetic. Relentless. I don't care about the kind of work as long as it gets done."

At Nostalgia Institute I wasn't just a designer — I was the whole operation. Content writing, design, photography, videography, editing. Literally an agency inside one person. I also co-established ISOAcademy, doing brand consultancy alongside it. This is who I am.
